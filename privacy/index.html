Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Telegram Bot: We collect your Telegram user ID and username when you interact with our bot. Submitted knowledge content and voting history are stored in our database.

Website: We use GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub may log standard web server information (IP address, browser type, pages visited). We do not set cookies or run analytics trackers at this time.

Blockchain: $MINDAI token transactions are recorded on the Solana blockchain and are publicly visible. We do not control or store this data.

2. How We Use Information

Telegram data is used to operate the knowledge submission and voting system. We never sell or share personal information with third parties.

3. Data Retention

Knowledge submissions and voting records are retained as part of the project's knowledge base. To request removal of your data, contact the team via Telegram.

4. Third-Party Services

We use: GitHub (website hosting), Telegram (community + bot), Solana blockchain (token), and pump.fun/Raydium (token exchange). Each service has its own privacy policy.

5. Changes
