Architecture

How the Data Network Works

No fluff. No promises we can't keep. Here's the architecture.

📋 Task System

Tasks are created by the platform (Phase 1-2) and later by enterprise clients (Phase 3+). Each task has clear instructions, examples, and quality criteria. Tasks cover crypto-specific domains:

  • Concept explanations (50-100 words, crypto & AI topics)
  • Transaction classification (transfer, swap, contract, NFT, MEV)
  • Project assessment (real project, suspicious, or scam)

✅ Cross-Verification

Every task is assigned to 5 annotators. The majority answer becomes the accepted ground truth. Annotators who match the majority earn full rewards. Partial matches earn partial rewards. Mismatches earn nothing and lose reputation.

  • 5 annotators per task — majority decides
  • Reputation score tracks accuracy over time
  • High-reputation annotators unlock premium tasks

💰 Revenue Distribution

When enterprises buy data, the revenue flows through a transparent smart contract:

  • 40% → Annotator rewards (paid in $MINDAI)
  • 20% → Market buyback of $MINDAI (not publicly announced)
  • 20% → Treasury (operations, development)
  • 20% → Operating costs (servers, tools, marketing)

Fair question. Most AI tokens are a whitepaper and a dream. We're different because we don't promise AGI — we build a data marketplace. Phase 1 is the token. Phase 2 (task system) launches next week. Revenue comes from enterprise data sales, not speculation. We ship before we hype.
"How do you ensure data quality?" ▼
Every annotation is cross-verified by 5 different people. If the majority agrees, it passes. Plus: (1) staking $MINDAI to annotate — spam costs money. (2) Reputation system — bad annotators lose access to premium tasks. (3) In Phase 4+, AI itself helps flag low-quality annotations.
