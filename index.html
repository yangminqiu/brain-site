Whitepaper

How MIND AI Actually Works

No fluff. No promises we can't keep. Here's the architecture.

🎯 The Base Model

We use open-source LLMs (DeepSeek, Qwen, or similar) as the foundation. No training required — these models already understand language. Our job is to make them knowledgeable about what the community cares about.

  • Self-hosted open-source base (Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama)
  • Zero GPU training — RAG + LoRA only
  • Model can be swapped via community vote

🧠 Knowledge Base (RAG)

RAG = Retrieval-Augmented Generation. instead of retraining the model, we build a community-curated database of knowledge. When you ask a question, the AI searches this database first, then generates an answer from the best sources.

  • All submitted content goes through community voting
  • Accepted content is vectorized and indexed
  • AI cites sources — transparency built in

🔧 Fine-Tuning (LoRA)

LoRA is a technique that adapts large models with very little compute. A single RTX 5090 (yes, the one in our office) can run a LoRA fine-tune in hours. As the knowledge base grows, we periodically fine-tune the model to internalize common community knowledge.

  • Runs on consumer hardware (RTX 5090, ~2 days)
  • LoRA weights are small — can be stored on-chain
  • No billion-dollar GPU cluster required

💰 Token Flow

Every action in the ecosystem circulates $MINDAI. Submit? Costs a little. Get accepted? Earn more. Vote? You're rewarded. Use the AI? Pay in $MINDAI — fees support the infrastructure.

  • Submit knowledge: costs 1 $MINDAI
  • Accepted submission: earn 50-500 $MINDAI
  • Vote on submissions: earn 0.1 $MINDAI / vote
  • AI query: costs 1 $MINDAI

FAQ

Questions People Actually Ask

We get it. Crypto is full of scams. Here's the honest answers.

"Isn't this just another AI token with no real product?" ▼
Fair question. Most AI tokens are a whitepaper and a dream. We're different because we don't need to build an AI from scratch — we use existing open-source models and layer community knowledge on top. Phase 1 is the token and community. Phase 2 is the knowledge submission system (working prototype within 2 weeks of launch). Phase 3 is the live AI. We ship before we hype.
"No GPU? How can you build AI without GPU?" ▼
We don't train models from scratch. Open-source models are pre-trained and freely available. We host them ourselves — no API dependency. What we add is: (1) a community-curated knowledge base through RAG, and (2) periodic LoRA fine-tuning on consumer hardware (RTX 5090). A single GPU is all we need.
"How do you prevent low-quality submissions?" ▼
Three layers: (1) submitting costs $MINDAI — spam is expensive. (2) Community voting filters quality — if you submit garbage, it won't get approved and you wasted your tokens. (3) In Phase 3+, the AI itself helps review submissions for quality. Only high-quality, community-verified content enters the knowledge base.
"What stops the team from rugging?" ▼
Team tokens (10%) are locked for 12 months via smart contract. Liquidity pool tokens will be public so anyone can verify they're locked. All major decisions go through community vote from Phase 1 (basic voting) to Phase 4 (full DAO). Our names and faces are public. A rug would destroy any value we've built — and we've been building for months.
"Why Solana? Why pump.fun?" ▼
Solana for speed and low fees — we want knowledge submissions to cost pennies, not dollars. Pump.fun for fair launch — no private sales, no VC allocations, no insider presale. Everyone buys at the same price from the same curve. And once we graduate to Raydium, liquidity is locked and visible to everyone.
"I have no special knowledge. Can I still participate?" ▼
Absolutely. You can: (1) Vote on submissions — earn $MINDAI for curating. (2) Use the AI — every query supports the ecosystem. (3) Hold and govern — your voice counts regardless of expertise.
