Building the Data Layer for Crypto AI

v2.1 · May 29, 2026 · $MINDAI

Abstract

MIND AI is building the data layer for Crypto AI — a community-powered infrastructure that produces, verifies, and delivers high-quality labeled data for the crypto industry. Think Scale AI for crypto, but community-owned.

The data annotation market is valued at over $10B and growing. Scale AI alone is valued at $13B+. Yet crypto-specific data barely exists. MIND AI fills this gap.

1. The Opportunity

AI systems need labeled data to function. In the enterprise world, companies like Scale AI have built billion-dollar businesses providing this data layer. The crypto industry is at the same inflection point:

  • → AI-powered crypto tools are emerging — trading bots, risk analysis, compliance
  • → These tools need labeled data specific to crypto — transactions, protocols, projects
  • → This data barely exists today, and no one is building it at scale

The data layer is the missing piece. MIND AI is building it.

2. The Model

MIND AI is a community-powered data network. The model is simple:

  • → Produce — People who understand crypto contribute their knowledge to the network
  • → Verify — Data is validated by the community, ensuring quality and accuracy
  • → Deliver — Verified datasets are made available to enterprises and developers
  • → Reward — Value flows back to the contributors and token holders who make it possible
