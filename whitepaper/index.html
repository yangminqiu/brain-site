MIND AI Whitepaper

Community-Powered Data Network for Crypto AI

Version 2.0 · May 29, 2026 · $MINDAI

Abstract

MIND AI is a community-powered data annotation network built on Solana. It connects crypto-knowledgeable contributors who label and verify data with enterprises that need high-quality training datasets for their AI systems. The network uses a fair launch token ($MINDAI) for staking, rewards, and governance.

Like Scale AI, but community-owned.

1. The Problem

AI systems in crypto need labeled data — but high-quality crypto-specific datasets barely exist. Traditional data annotation is:

  • Centralized — dominated by a handful of companies that control pricing and quality
  • Expensive — enterprise annotation costs $0.50-$5 per item, pricing out smaller projects
  • Not crypto-native — general annotators don't understand DeFi, MEV, or Solana transactions
  • Unfair — workers earn pennies while platforms keep the margin

The $10B+ data annotation industry has a crypto-shaped hole. MIND AI fills it.

2. The Solution: Community Data Network

MIND AI replaces centralized annotation with community-powered cross-verification:

  • Task Creation — Platform or enterprises create annotation tasks with clear instructions
  • Community Annotation — Contributors pick tasks and submit answers
  • Cross-Verification — 5 annotators per task; majority decides ground truth
  • Rewards — Accepted annotations earn $MINDAI; quality builds reputation
  • Data Packaging — Verified data is compiled into datasets for enterprise sale

💡 Task Types (Phase 2)

  • Concept Explanation — Explain crypto/AI concepts in 50-100 words
  • Transaction Classification — Categorize Solana transactions (transfer, swap, contract, NFT, MEV)
  • Project Assessment — Evaluate project descriptions as real/suspicious/scam

3. Tokenomics: $MINDAI

$MINDAI is a utility token that powers the data network. Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens, never minted again.

Fair Launch

$MINDAI launched on pump.fun (May 24, 2026). Zero pre-allocation. No VC round. No private sale. Everyone — including the team — buys from the same bonding curve at the same price.

  • 🟢 Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 $MINDAI
  • 🟢 Team: 200M purchased at market price (100M for community rewards, 100M locked on StakePoint for 12 months)
  • 🟢 Contract: 9xSirb8EkpPg1EsUPBeeeM9GYS7tnZZZXLbAMScJpump

Token Utility

  • Staking — Lock $MINDAI to access annotation tasks (anti-cheat mechanism)
  • Rewards — Earn $MINDAI for accepted annotations and accurate cross-verification
  • Reputation — Higher reputation unlocks premium tasks with better rewards
  • Governance — Hold $MINDAI to vote on network parameters and data pricing
  • Enterprise Payment — Buy datasets by paying $MINDAI (or USDC → buyback)

Revenue Distribution

When enterprises purchase datasets, revenue is distributed transparently:

  • 40% → Annotator rewards (distributed in $MINDAI)
  • 20% → Market buyback of $MINDAI (not publicly announced)
  • 20% → Treasury (development, operations, future growth)
  • 20% → Operating costs (servers, tools, marketing, community management)

4. Quality Assurance

Data quality is ensured through a multi-layered system:

  • Cross-Verification — 5 annotators per task; majority agreement is required for acceptance
  • Staking — Annotators must stake $MINDAI; bad annotations result in slashing
  • Reputation — Accuracy tracked over time; low reputation limits task access
  • AI-Assisted Review — In Phase 4+, AI flags low-quality annotations for manual review
  • Enterprise Validation — Datasets include quality reports; buyers can challenge flagged items

5. Economic Flywheel

🌱 Layer 1 — Data Generation

Community annotates data → earns $MINDAI → more people join → more data produced → better datasets.

🏢 Layer 2 — Enterprise Revenue

Enterprises buy datasets → pay $MINDAI → 40% to annotators, 20% market buyback → token demand increases.

💎 Layer 3 — Value Flywheel

Enterprise revenue → Market buyback → Token scarcity 📈 → More annotators → Better data → More clients → Revenue grows → Cycle repeats.
