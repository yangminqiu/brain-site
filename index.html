FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BRAIN.

What can $BRAIN be used for? ▼
Holding $BRAIN gives you governance rights (voting on AI learning direction), the ability to submit knowledge content, earn rewards from community voting, and future priority access to AI services. It is the core of the ecosystem.
Where does the AI come from? Do I need to train my own? ▼
We use mature open-source large language models (DeepSeek, Qwen) as the base, enhanced by the community-contributed knowledge base. No need to train your own model. Phase 3+ may introduce community-driven fine-tuning.
How do I participate in governance? ▼
Hold $BRAIN and you're in. Governance includes voting on AI learning topics, curating submitted knowledge, and (in Phase 4) proposing and voting on AI behavior parameters, model choices, and more.
What gives $BRAIN its value? ▼
Supply and demand. The more active the community and the better the AI, the more people want to participate, driving demand for $BRAIN. Each AI call burns tokens, reducing supply. Value grows with ecosystem health.
How do I buy $BRAIN? ▼
After deployment, $BRAIN will have liquidity on a decentralized exchange. You can buy via pump.fun or Raydium using a Solana wallet. Links will be posted on the website and Telegram community.
Is this a rug pull? ▼
No. Team tokens are locked for 12 months. Liquidity will not be removed arbitrarily. All major decisions go through community vote. Our success depends on your trust and the ecosystem we build together.
